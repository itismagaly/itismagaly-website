Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Who we are

This website, magalybustamante.com (“itismagaly”), is run by Magaly Bustamante, an independent journalist and content creator based between Cyprus and Hungary. For any privacy question, contact itismagaly@gmail.com.

What data is collected

Contact form

When you submit the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent through Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, directly to Magaly’s inbox. This data is used only to respond to your inquiry and is not used for marketing without your separate consent.

Analytics

If you accept analytics cookies, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, approximate location (derived from IP address), device/browser type, and referral source. Analytics only loads after you click “Accept” on the cookie banner; if you click “Decline”, no analytics script or cookie is set.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is sent directly to and stored by Buttondown, a third-party newsletter service, which Magaly uses to send you newsletter issues. Your email is used only for this purpose and you can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email.

Legal basis for processing

  • Analytics cookies are set only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via the “Cookie Settings” link in the footer.
  • Contact form data is processed based on your request and Magaly’s legitimate interest in responding to it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Third parties & international transfers

Data may be processed by Web3Forms (contact form delivery), Buttondown (newsletter delivery), and Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC (Analytics), which may transfer data outside the European Economic Area. These providers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers.

Data retention

Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry. Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months before automatic deletion.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email itismagaly@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the site or legal requirements. The date at the top shows the last revision.

Qué datos se recopilan

Formulario de contacto

Cuando envías el formulario de contacto, tu nombre, correo electrónico y mensaje se envían mediante Web3Forms, un servicio externo de envío de formularios, directamente a la bandeja de entrada de Magaly. Si aceptas las cookies de analítica, este sitio usa Google Analytics 4 para entender cómo se usa el sitio — páginas visitadas, ubicación aproximada (derivada de la IP), tipo de dispositivo/navegador y fuente de referencia. La analítica solo se activa después de que hagas clic en “Aceptar” en el aviso de cookies; si haces clic en “Rechazar”, no se carga ningún script ni cookie de analítica.

Newsletter

Si te suscribes al newsletter, tu correo electrónico se envía directamente y se almacena en Buttondown, un servicio externo de newsletters que Magaly usa para enviarte las ediciones. Tu correo se usa solo para este fin y puedes darte de baja en cualquier momento con el enlace en cada email.

Base legal del tratamiento

  • Las cookies de analítica solo se activan con tu consentimiento (Art. 6(1)(a) RGPD), que puedes retirar en cualquier momento con el enlace “Configuración de Cookies” en el pie de página.

Retención de datos

Los mensajes del formulario de contacto se conservan solo el tiempo necesario para atender tu consulta. Los datos de Google Analytics se conservan durante 14 meses antes de eliminarse automáticamente.

Tus derechos

Bajo el RGPD, tienes derecho a acceder, corregir, eliminar o exportar tus datos, a oponerte o restringir su tratamiento, y a retirar tu consentimiento en cualquier momento. Para ejercer estos derechos, escribe a itismagaly@gmail.com. También puedes presentar una queja ante tu autoridad local de protección de datos.
